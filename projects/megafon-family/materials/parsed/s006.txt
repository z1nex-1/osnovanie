Add / Remove Family Line Member
Easily manage your shared Family Line group by adding or removing members, up to 5 total. Update your group as family needs change, ensuring the right people are always connected to your shared line.
Before you begin
Ensure the Verizon Family app is installed on the Guardian's device.
Sign in to the app using a My Verizon user ID or mobile number that has Account Owner or Manager access privileges.
Steps
Open the Verizon Family Guardian app.
Tap the
Chat / Call tab
.
Select the
Settings icon
.
Tap
Manage
.
Do one of the following:
To add a member:
Tap
Invite members
.
Tap the desired Family member then tap
Send Invite
.
If prompted, tap
Agree
.
To remove a member:
Tap the
Trash icon
to the far right of the Family member you wish to remove.
Review the message then tap
Remove
or
Leave
.
